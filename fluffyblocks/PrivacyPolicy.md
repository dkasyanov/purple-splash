# Privacy Policy — Fluffy Blocks

_Last updated: 22 July 2026_

Fluffy Blocks ("the app") is developed by Purple Splash ("we", "us"). This
policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: **the app does
not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## Data we collect

None. Fluffy Blocks does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or
sensitive information about you.

## Network access

The app does not use the internet. It contains no analytics, no advertising,
and no third-party services or SDKs. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your
device.

## Local data

The app saves your high score and your sound/haptics preferences on your own
device so they persist between sessions. This information stays on your device,
is never transmitted, and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app requests a single permission — **Vibrate** — used only for in-game
haptic feedback. It requests no other permissions.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it is safe for players of all
ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
new "Last updated" date.
